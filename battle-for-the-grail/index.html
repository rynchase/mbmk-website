The quest

Each land has its own creatures, its own weapon, and a boss waiting at the end.

LEVEL 1

The Village and Forest

Leave the village with a double-ended spear and fight through bears, wolves and sneaky foxes.

Boss: the Giant Tree Monster and his enormous green hammer.

LEVEL 2

The Sea Voyage

Hold the deck with sword and shield while the ship sails on — and watch out for rogue waves.

Boss: the Kraken.

LEVEL 3

The Bog

Squelch through a glowing, gloomy swamp full of things that bite.

Boss: the Huge Fire-Spitting Frog.

LEVEL 4

The Desert

Cross hot sand, cacti and rocky canyons under a huge blue sky.

Boss: the Mummy.

LEVEL 5

The Mountain

Climb rope nets up an icy mountain, past little red dragons, to the very top.

Boss: the Giant Black Dragon — guardian of the Grail.

What's inside

🗡️

Five worlds

Forest, sea, bog, desert and mountain — each with its own enemies and weapon.

🐉

Five bosses

Every boss gets angrier at half health. Learn their moves to win.

👆

Made for touch

Simple on-screen D-pad, jump and attack buttons for phones and tablets.

🚩

Checkpoints

Ten lives per level and mid-level checkpoints, so younger players can keep going.

🙅

No ads, no purchases

No pop-ups, no in-app purchases, no accounts. Just the game.

✈️

Plays offline

No internet needed. Progress is saved on your device.

Find the Grail

Beat the dragon, break the diamond case, and bring the Holy Grail home. The real Grail turns out to be a humble wooden cup.
