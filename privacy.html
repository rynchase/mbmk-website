Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy covers all games published by Made By My Kids, including Battle for the Grail, on Android and iOS. Our games are made for kids and families, so we keep this simple: we don't collect your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Our games:

  • do not ask for your name, email, age, location or any other personal information;
  • do not have accounts or sign-in;
  • do not show ads and do not include advertising or analytics SDKs;
  • do not have in-app purchases;
  • do not need an internet connection to play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, high score and settings (like sound volume) on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children's privacy

Because we don't collect any personal information from anyone, we don't collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Third parties

Games are downloaded through Google Play and the Apple App Store, which have their own privacy policies. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example, if a future game adds an online feature — we'll update this page before that game is released and change the date at the top.
