How it works

The kids are the game designers. We just do the typing.

1

The kids write the story

Who's the hero? What's the quest? Which monster lives in the swamp? Every big idea comes from them.

2

We build it together

Their story becomes levels, bosses and pixel art — and they playtest every version and tell us what's too easy.

3

You get to play it

The finished game goes to Google Play and the App Store, so other kids (and grown-ups) can play it too.
